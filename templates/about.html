About Our Project

Breaking Communication Barriers with Technology

Our Sign Language Detector project aims to reduce communication barriers between deaf and hearing individuals using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Welcome to Sign Language Detector!

Sign Language Detector is an AI-based web application that recognizes hand gestures through a camera and converts them into meaningful text and speech. Our application helps people communicate easily by recognizing sign language alphabets and words in real time.

Key Features

Real-time Detection

Detects hand gestures using your camera in real time.

Alphabet Recognition

Recognizes sign language alphabets from A to Z.

Word Recognition

Identifies different sign language words.

Text to Speech

Converts recognized text into speech.

Sentence Builder

Creates meaningful sentences from detected alphabets.

Sign Learning

Helps users learn different sign language gestures.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make communication easier, faster and more accessible for everyone through Artificial Intelligence technology.
